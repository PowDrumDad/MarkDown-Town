# Lesson 1: Quiz Solutions

Here are the answers to the quiz:

**Question 1:** How do you create a main heading in Markdown?
**Answer:** b) `# Heading`

**Question 2:** How do you make text italic?
**Answer:** b) `*text*`

**Question 3:** What is the purpose of Markdown?
**Answer:** c) To add formatting to plain text in a simple way.

**Question 4:** To create a paragraph, you just need to...
**Answer:** c) Separate lines of text with a blank line.
