# Lesson 2: Lists and Links

In this lesson, we'll learn how to organize information using lists and how to connect our documents to the wider web with links.

## Organizing with Lists

Lists are perfect for structuring information, like a series of steps, a checklist of items, or a collection of related points.

### Unordered Lists

An unordered list is a simple bulleted list. You can create one by starting a line with an asterisk (`*`), a hyphen (`-`), or a plus sign (`+`).

```markdown
*   Somenos Marsh
*   Cowichan River
*   Maple Bay
```

Renders as:
*   Somenos Marsh
*   Cowichan River
*   Maple Bay

### Ordered Lists

An ordered list is a numbered list. You create one by starting a line with a number followed by a period.

```markdown
1.  Check water temperature.
2.  Collect water sample.
3.  Record observations.
```

Renders as:
1.  Check water temperature.
2.  Collect water sample.
3.  Record observations.

## Connecting with Links

Links allow you to direct your readers to other web pages or documents. The syntax for a link is `[Link Text](URL)`.

-   The `Link Text` is what the reader will see and click on.
-   The `URL` is the web address the link points to.

For example:
`Visit the [Land Stewards official website](https://www.example.com).`

Renders as:
Visit the [Land Stewards official website](https://www.example.com).
