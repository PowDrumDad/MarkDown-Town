# Lesson 1: Practice

**Your Assignment:**

The Land Stewards need a new report on the Cowichan River. Your task is to create the header for this report.

1.  Create a main heading (Level 1) with the title: `Cowichan River Survey`.
2.  Create a sub-heading (Level 2) with the date: `December 15, 2025`.
3.  Write a short introductory paragraph. Here's some text you can use, but feel free to write your own:
    `This report details the initial findings of our weekly survey of the Cowichan River. The primary focus of this survey is to monitor water quality and observe the local wildlife.`
4.  In the paragraph, make the words `Cowichan River` **bold**.
5.  Make the words `water quality` and `local wildlife` *italic*.

Good luck!
